# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CowBot welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participants should be able to contribute without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or unwanted disclosure of private information.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, use welcoming language, give constructive feedback, and assume good intent while taking concerns seriously. Discuss ideas and evidence; respect different perspectives and accept responsibility when your words or actions cause harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish private communications or personal information without consent.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer using a private contact method they have published. Do not post private incident details in public issues. If no private method is available, request a private contact channel in a neutral issue without naming affected people or describing sensitive details. The project does not currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting address.

Maintainers should handle reports with discretion, hear relevant context, and recuse themselves when personally involved. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a request to correct behavior, a warning, removal of content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the reason to the affected participant where practical. A participant may request reconsideration by an uninvolved maintainer where one is available.
